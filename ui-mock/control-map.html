Implementation hand-off

The redesign may regroup controls; it must not thin them out.

  • Keep stable behaviour names. Production actions can map to new component placement without losing intent.
  • Keep conditional reasons visible. A disabled action needs the same truthful gate and recovery route.
  • Keep reviews atomic. Import, publication, migration, conflict and destruction remain explicit boundaries.
  • Keep provenance near decisions. Canonical ownership, source lineage and where-used evidence stay inspectable.
